# ALPHA-008 Validation

## Scope

ALPHA-008 removes the false choice between rescanning the entire Bulletproof lake
for every research cycle and restricting discovery to previously admitted BTC data.
Discovery consumes the immutable manifest catalog. Only the exact panels selected
before outcomes are opened, hashed, quality checked and materialized through native
DATA-001/002/003 records. Raw lake bytes remain read-only and outside model prompts.

The weekly mandate also freezes a native strategy-capability catalog at the exact
Bulletproof commit. A senior researcher may name a reusable hypothesis only when the
registered strategy, timeframe, input cardinality, logging contract and finite grid
match the proposed mechanism. Otherwise the question enters the existing explicit
strategy-engineering gate. No approximate strategy substitution is allowed.

## Verified Source Evidence

- Native ALPHA admission v2 binds canonical path, bytes, schema, identity, exact
  one-minute continuity, coverage manifests and a verified same-host recovery copy.
- A batch admission command accepts only the preregistered unique panel set, bounded
  to 20 assets, and grants no execution, order, capital or promotion authority.
- Hermes creates a risk-zero lazy-admission task under the approved weekly mandate,
  registers native receipts as DATA-001/002/003 evidence, and resumes the immutable
  candidate through a separate admission ledger rather than mutating it.
- Campaign and worker contracts preserve every panel binding, the primary instrument,
  preregistered basket, research timeframe and right-closed, left-labeled complete-bar
  resampling policy.
- Existing native strategies are catalogued from registered YAML contracts. Reuse is
  allowed only at the frozen contract digest, within eight variants, with exact truth
  semantics and an explicit logging contract. Pipeline smoke fixtures are explicitly
  ineligible for research reuse. Existing strategies are currently
  single-instrument; a mixed-basket hypothesis is valid research input but cannot be
  silently executed on its first panel and instead requires bounded engineering.
- Registered-contract cards bind the original YAML digest and represent its internals
  by reference. They do not invent a second feature transcription. Novel questions
  cannot fall back to the weekend-momentum strategy.
- The dedicated `invariance-swarm-alpha-data-admission.service` consumes only
  `alpha_data_admission` leases. Its root-owned environment is bound to the
  `vm1-alpha-data-admission` identity; the Bulletproof primary checkout and lake are
  read-only, while task worktrees and content-addressed recovery evidence are the only
  writable data paths. Install it only after bootstrap with
  `worker/systemd/install-alpha-data-admission.sh`.

## Claim Boundary And Rollback

Catalog visibility does not prove data quality or authorize execution. Selected-panel
admission does not prove alpha. Registered implementation does not prove that a new
question is scientifically equivalent; the senior researcher must bind it explicitly
and independent review remains mandatory. No shadow, order or capital authority is
added.

Rollback stops new ALPHA data-admission leases and new mandate activation, preserves
all catalog, candidate, receipt and DATA records, and restores the prior single-panel
campaign path. Existing immutable approvals are never broadened or rewritten.
