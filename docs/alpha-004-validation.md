Source validation demonstrates:

- immutable, seven-day maximum no-capital mandates and digest-safe founder approval;
- independently chartered RI synthesis, senior hypothesis-generation and
  outcome-blind data-representation tasks;
- fixed read-only Codex execution with heartbeats and bounded structured output;
- predictive, falsifiability, evidence, equation, DATA-002/003 and duplicate gates;
- DISC-009 allocation and ALPHA/BT-009 campaign replenishment;
- mandate-scoped removal of redundant hypothesis and execution approvals;
- continued explicit approval for code, expanded data, shadow and capital actions;
- negative, invalid and failed attempt feedback into later discovery context;
- hypothesis-specific multi-instrument Bybit/Binance baskets and arbitrary
  whole-minute/hour/day research timeframes, bounded by immutable catalog and
  admitted-dataset evidence;
- Mission Control mandate, agent, throughput, cycle and stall visibility;
- one canonical active weekly mandate with parent-bound thematic exceptions;
- a rolling 365-day primary window ending at the newest complete DATA-002 bar and
  separately contracted survivor validation up to 1,095 days;
- reasoned 1-100 asset selection (schema ceiling 128) with deterministic large-basket
  overlap, liquidity, admission and compute gates;
- low-watermark replenishment across up to three active campaigns; and
- a typed native mechanism catalog, including CSI components and provenance, exposed
  as falsifiable prior designs rather than assumed signals.

Targeted source tests cover existing campaign compatibility, portfolio admission,
mandate bounds, delegation requirements, predictive rejection and equation
verification. The representation stage must cover every frozen candidate exactly,
cannot change its question or mechanism, never receives outcome data, records rejected
alternatives, and binds Bulletproof's right-closed, left-labeled, complete-bar
resampler. Production completion additionally requires migration, four-service
installation, one approved mandate, fresh agent heartbeats and one terminal campaign
attempt.

Focused validation passes 166 backend and 35 worker tests for mandate, campaign,
scheduler, representation and external-research contracts. A native Bulletproof test
separately proves that CSI's component definition, available sources and falsification
notes survive catalog publication. These tests prove contracts and enforcement, not
the quality of an LLM's future market judgment.

ALPHA-004 does not claim alpha, profitability, shadow readiness or capital
authority. It makes the scientific loop continuous and observable.
