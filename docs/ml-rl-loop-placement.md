# ML and RL Placement in the Research Loop

## Current truth

ML-002/003/004 and RL-001/002 have native producer and control-path evidence, but the
continuous ALPHA loop does not automatically route every BT-009 result through them.
Registry completion is not continuous integration.

## ML branch

ML is optional and begins only after a causal signal/state contract exists. ML-002
materializes point-in-time features, labels, purged/embargoed splits and train-only
fitted transforms. ML-003 evaluates model families and regimes against unconditional
and linear baselines on identical folds. ML-004 calibrates uncertainty and creates
abstention behavior. A qualified model then rejoins the ordinary cost, portfolio,
independent-review, shadow and promotion gates. Simple transparent rules need not be
forced through ML.

## RL branch

RL begins later, after enough immutable historical or shadow decisions contain state,
action, reward, behavior-policy propensity, support, fills and cost lineage. RL-001
defines and audits that offline dataset. RL-002 performs conservative off-policy
evaluation under uncertainty and support stress. RL-003 remains the planned learned
sizing/allocation/execution sandbox beneath deterministic risk controls. There is no
online capital exploration and no learned policy may override RISK-005 or EXEC-007.

## Missing router

A future eligibility router must classify terminal signal, strategy and shadow receipts
and create ML/RL proposals only when their prerequisites exist. It must retain an
explicit `not_applicable` outcome, preserve final OOS separation and require a new
registered trial for retraining. Until that router is deployed, ML and RL are governed
capabilities available to the laboratory, not automatic stages of every campaign.
