# RISK-001 drawdown, tail and reverse-stress runbook

1. Pin the PORT-001 candidate, portfolio state, Bulletproof run, cost model, and scenario-pack digests.
2. Produce drawdown-path and tail-uncertainty receipts from the immutable run.
3. Evaluate all five required failure mechanisms and bracket their reverse-stress thresholds.
4. Register the request. The server derives the immutable dossier and blocks stale or breached evidence.
5. Replay the stored assessment by ID and verify its dossier digest.

Rollback restores the prior versioned scenario pack, but its evidence age is re-evaluated. A stale prior pack remains blocked. This service cannot allocate capital or place orders.
